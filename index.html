What makes it different

Built around how you actually cook

Not a calorie tracker. Not a meal planner. Not an algorithm. Just your recipes — exactly as you'd write them down.

Your personal notebook

Add recipes the way you'd write them: a name, ingredients, steps, a photo. No mandatory fields, no endless forms. While cooking, the recipe stays in front of you. While shopping, your ingredient list is right there.

Shared with your family

Create a family space, invite people via a link or tag, and everyone's recipes live in one shared notebook. Grandma's recipe stays in the family — not buried in a WhatsApp message or a yellowed scrap of paper.

Social when you want it

Publish a recipe and let others discover it. Like and save what inspires you. Or keep everything private — it's entirely your choice. No pressure to post. No feed to scroll.

Families

The recipes that belong to all of you

A family in Uculi is a shared recipe space with a name and a PIN. Invite anyone via WhatsApp or a link. Every member sees the full collection — and everyone can contribute.

Families are not mandatory. You don't have to be social if you don't want to. But the infrastructure is there the moment you do.

  • Invite via WhatsApp, link or tag
  • Everyone can add and view recipes
  • Works across languages — NL, EN, FR, DE, ES
  • Preserved forever — no expiry, no data loss

Every family has a story. Now they have a place to keep it.

Recipes are not data.
They are memories.

The smell of a dish calls up a memory that words cannot. But the words — the ingredients, the steps, the small note "don't bake too long or it dries out" — those words are all that remains when someone is no longer here.

Uculi wants to be the place where those words are kept. Not in a drawer. Not on an anonymous server. In a notebook that belongs to you and your family — that goes where you go and is never lost.

That is not a marketing line. That is why this app exists.
