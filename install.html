Add Uculi to your home screen

Open Uculi like an app on your phone. No extra store install is required.

  1. Open Uculi in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android).
  2. Use Share / Add to Home Screen (or Install app).
  3. Confirm — Uculi appears on your home screen.
